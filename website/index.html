Everything for your
next sign-in.

A familiar home for your verification codes, with room to make it yours.

✧

Your vault, on your PC

Authenticator keys are stored in an encrypted local vault. Generate time-based verification codes even without an internet connection.

☁

Sync when you want to

Optional Google Drive sync encrypts your account data before upload. A small status badge keeps you informed.

↥

A way back home

Export encrypted backups, see your last backup, and choose gentle reminders. Help & Recovery walks you through moving PCs and restoring accounts.

▦

A place for every key

Drag accounts into your preferred order, mark favorites, and create folders with your own names and colors. Supported services have familiar offline logos.

☀

Snow light. Polar night.

Choose a bright snowfield or a quiet winter evening. A snowy pine scene follows your theme, with motion that respects your system preferences.

Your language, instantly

Switch between English, Bahasa Indonesia, and 日本語 in Settings. Glacia updates without restarting.
