Privacy policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Glacia Authenticator is a Windows app created by Enochi Sasaina as part of Winter Garden Project. Your authenticator keys are kept in a local encrypted vault. Google Drive sync is optional. This policy describes the current app and this website; the public installer is still in preparation.

1. Information on your PC

Glacia stores the authenticator setup secrets needed to generate your codes, account names and labels, folder membership, favorites, and other vault data on your PC. The vault uses AES-256-GCM encryption with a key derived from your vault password using scrypt. Keys are available in memory while the vault is unlocked.

Preferences such as language, theme, startup, and backup reminders are stored locally outside the encrypted vault. If you choose to keep your vault logged in, a derived vault key is saved using Windows-protected storage. Google connection information and tokens also use Windows-protected storage. These safeguards depend on the security of your Windows account and device.

Glacia does not operate a developer-hosted service for collecting your vault or authenticator secrets. The current app includes no advertising, usage analytics, or automatic crash-report upload.

2. Optional Google sign-in and Drive sync

You can use a local vault without signing in to Google. If you connect Google, Glacia requests your account identifier and email to identify the connected account, and access to its own private application-data folder in Google Drive. This access is used for Glacia's encrypted cloud vault snapshots and restore functions. It does not provide general access to your Drive documents or Google Authenticator's private cloud backups.

Google handles sign-in in your browser. Glacia receives authorization tokens, not your Google account password. Access and refresh tokens are stored locally using Windows-protected storage. Google processes the sign-in and Drive requests under Google's privacy policy.

Vault account data is encrypted on your PC with a separate sync key before upload. Your sync password is not uploaded as plaintext. Drive still receives file metadata, including identifiers and the name of your cloud vault; cloud vault names and other file metadata are not encrypted as vault contents. Avoid putting sensitive information in a cloud vault name.

When sync access is remembered, Glacia stores a protected sync-key record tied to the Google account and cloud vault. This allows the same account to resume sync after reopening or signing in again. A different Google account does not reuse the previous account's sync access. Locking or signing out clears active access, but protected sync records may remain locally for later reconnection.

Google data is used to provide sign-in, encrypted backup, sync, and restore. It is not sold or used for advertising. Glacia's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

3. Imports, backups, and your clipboard

Setup keys, authenticator links, and QR images you choose to import are processed on your PC. Glacia does not upload your QR images to an external scanning service. Encrypted backup files are saved to the location you choose, protected by the backup password you provide.

Plaintext exports and setup QR codes contain authenticator secrets. Anyone with those secrets can generate the same codes. Share them only through a method you trust. A folder you choose may be backed up by another service according to that service's settings.

When you copy a verification code, Glacia places it on the Windows clipboard and attempts to clear it after about 30 seconds if it is still the current clipboard text. Windows clipboard history, clipboard sync, or other apps may retain a copy.

4. Retention, disconnection, and deletion

Local data stays on your PC until you remove it. You can delete individual accounts in Glacia or remove the local app data after keeping any backups you need. Uninstalling or moving a portable app should not be assumed to erase its separate user-data folder.

Locking the vault and signing out do not delete your keys or cloud backups. Disconnecting Google stops the active connection and removes its saved sign-in tokens, but existing Drive files and protected settings for resuming sync may remain. You can revoke Glacia's Google access from your Google account and remove its hidden application data through Google Drive's app settings.

Deleting a key updates the active vault and can propagate through enabled sync. Independent backup files, older device copies, and older cloud snapshots may still contain that key. Remove those copies separately when you no longer need them. Revoking a setup secret at its original service is the way to invalidate that secret.

The developer cannot retrieve or erase vault data stored only on your PC or in your own Google Drive. Lost vault, backup, or sync passwords cannot be recovered by the developer.

5. Support messages

If you contact enochi3317@gmail.com, the developer receives the address and information you include. Messages are used to answer your request and troubleshoot reported issues, and may be retained for follow-up. The email provider processes messages under its own policy. Please do not send passwords, authenticator setup keys, sensitive QR codes, or unencrypted vault exports.

6. This website

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. It adds no analytics scripts, advertising, tracking cookies, or account forms. GitHub processes requests to host the website and logs visitor IP addresses for security. See the GitHub Privacy Statement and GitHub Pages documentation for the hosting provider's practices.

7. Changes and contact

This policy will be updated when Glacia's data practices change. The date above indicates the latest revision. Questions about this policy can be sent to enochi3317@gmail.com.
